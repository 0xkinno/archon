# ARCHON: Post-Hackathon Roadmap

## Vision: The Autonomous Physical Campus Operating System

### Phase A: Direct Building Management System (BMS) Hardware Connectors
- Native BACnet/IP and Modbus protocol adapters for live bi-directional sensor reading and automated valve shut-off.
- Certified integrations with Johnson Controls Metasys, Schneider Electric EcoStruxure, and Siemens Desigo CC.

### Phase B: Spatial Digital Twin & Multimodal Video Verification
- Integrate Google Gemini Multimodal Live API to ingest live CCTV security camera and thermal drone feeds during fire or water incidents.
- Spatial 3D BIM (Building Information Modeling) mapping to calculate exact water flood gradients and structural load risks.

### Phase C: Predictive Failure Analytics & Automated Spare Parts Procurement
- Predictive maintenance agents analyzing vibration and temperature trends to order critical HVAC pump seals before catastrophic failure.
- Direct ERP integration with SAP S/4HANA and Oracle Cloud SCM for automated purchase order issuance within pre-approved thresholds.
